# Home hero illustrations

The active hero uses six Kodama illustrations: the original standing `kodama-watercolor.png` plus sitting, upside-down, jumping, waving, and peeking variants. The five new assets were generated with the built-in image_gen tool and saved in this folder with their transparent alpha preserved. Their filenames and exact prompts are recorded in [KODAMA-PROMPTS.md](./KODAMA-PROMPTS.md).

The earlier individual Studio Ghibli drawings and their [WATERCOLOR-PROMPTS.md](./WATERCOLOR-PROMPTS.md) remain in the project. The two grouped illustrations below are also earlier versions.

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool for Thomas's requested Studio Ghibli character drawings. Both PNGs preserve the generated transparent alpha background.

## ghibli-left.png

Characters: Totoro, No-Face (Spirited Away), and Ponyo.

Final prompt:

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: transparent PNG decorative character group for the LEFT side of a personal portfolio website hero on white. Create exactly three recognizable Studio Ghibli characters as charming small hand-drawn colored-pencil and light watercolor illustrations, with slightly imperfect delicate outlines and soft muted colors: left to right Totoro (round gray body, cream belly with chevrons, pointed ears), No-Face from Spirited Away (tall slim black cloak and white oval mask with purple markings), and Ponyo (little cheerful red-haired fish-girl in her red dress). All three full bodies, separate with clear transparent gaps, no overlap. Totoro medium height, No-Face tallest, Ponyo shorter, all on a shared baseline. Compact horizontal group, roughly square canvas, art fills most of canvas with modest transparent padding. Facing forward or gently toward the center to the right. A whimsical sketchbook feel, clean enough to read at 250px overall width. True transparent alpha background, no ground plane, no scenery, no text, no labels, no logo, no sticker border, no extra characters or objects, no photographic realism. This is standalone new art, not a screenshot or a website mockup.

## ghibli-right.png

Characters: Kodama (Princess Mononoke), Calcifer (Howl's Moving Castle), and the Laputa gardener robot (Castle in the Sky).

Final prompt:

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: transparent PNG decorative character group for the RIGHT side of a personal portfolio website hero on white. Create exactly three recognizable Studio Ghibli characters as charming small hand-drawn colored-pencil and light watercolor illustrations, with slightly imperfect delicate outlines and soft muted colors: left to right one Kodama forest spirit from Princess Mononoke (tiny ivory body, round asymmetrical head with dark circular eyes and mouth), Calcifer from Howl's Moving Castle (small friendly orange-yellow flame with expressive eyes, resting on one little log), and the gentle Laputa gardener robot from Castle in the Sky (tall weathered olive-bronze robot, rounded head with two differently sized circular eyes, very long slender arms). All three full bodies, separate with clear transparent gaps, no overlap. Kodama short, Calcifer small, Laputa robot tallest, all on a shared baseline. Compact horizontal group, roughly square canvas, art fills most of canvas with modest transparent padding. Facing forward or gently toward the center to the left. A whimsical sketchbook feel, clean enough to read at 250px overall width; use delicate gray outlines on the ivory Kodama so it reads on white. True transparent alpha background, no ground plane, no scenery, no text, no labels, no logo, no sticker border, no extra characters or objects, no photographic realism. This is standalone new art, not a screenshot or a website mockup.
